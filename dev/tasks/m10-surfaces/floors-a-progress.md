## Painters (src/render/textures/packs/floors-a.ts)
- `board-floor`: boards or plank-shaped tiles from a tinted grain/print map: width patterns (uniform, mixed2-4), random, half, third, ship and stack bonds, bevel (square, micro, pillow, vgroove), caulked joints, nails, a 45 degree lay, painted finish, inlay lines.
- `wood-parquet`: herringbone (also double strips), chevron at any mitre angle, basket weave (also diagonal, also parallel checker), brick blocks, pinwheel, turned hexagons, Versailles panels. Grain follows each block.
- `rubber-relief` (renamed from rubber-floor: floors-c also registers `rubber-floor`): studs, coins, ribs, smooth speckle in tiles.
Layouts are periodic by construction (classes keyed mod the period), no drawWrapped needed.
