## New painters (src/render/textures/packs/walls-d.ts)
- `deco-geo`: styles sunray, skyline, ziggurat, arches, lattice, arcs, pilaster (7 designs).
- `play-geo`: bunting, confetti (2 designs). `quilt-set`: barn quilt Ohio-star blocks (1 design).

## fal.ai
58 FLUX.2 [dev] motif images, each fetched once (HTTP 429 responses on the first run were retried by re-running, not billed),
about $0.73 (1024x1024, $0.0126 each; see LEDGER.md). Within the cap of 60 images. All 58 built without rejection.
Motifs are reused across layouts only where the layout differs clearly (atomic and boomerang, rocket from the pilot).

## Review rounds
Round 1 sheets showed: skyline towers merged into bands, pearl lattice drawn only in one corner (line generation bug),
overlaps where a filler motif sat on brick-shifted rows (dino, safari, unicorn, celestial moved to `diagonal`, fox to `grid`),
oversized or overlapping scatter (coral, leopard, blocks, monsters, seashells), small bunting and fountain. All fixed in round 2.
